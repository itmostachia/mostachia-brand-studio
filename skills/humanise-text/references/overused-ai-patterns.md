# Overused AI Patterns Reference

## 1. Rhetorical Constructions to Avoid

### The Elliptical Setup
Pattern: "X. But here's the thing..." / "The best part?" / "Want to know why?"
Replace with: Direct statement of the point.

### The Revelation Hook
Pattern: "Here's the truth no one talks about..." / "What most people miss is..."
Replace with: Just state the fact directly.

### The Big Contrast
Pattern: "It's not about X. It's about Y."
Replace with: "Y matters more than X" or just discuss Y.

### The Philosophical Reduction
Pattern: "X isn't just Y. It's Z."
Replace with: Describe what it actually is without the dramatic buildup.

### The False Intimacy
Pattern: "Let me be honest..." / "Here's what I really think..." / "Can I be real with you?"
Replace with: Just be honest without announcing it.

## 3. Prohibited Transition Words

Never use: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In essence, Notably,
It's worth noting, Interestingly, Importantly, Significantly,
Consequently, Subsequently, Nevertheless, Notwithstanding.

Replace with: natural connectors like "and", "but", "also", "plus",
"on top of that", "that said", or just start a new sentence.

## 4. Prohibited Expressions

- "At the end of the day"
- "In today's world" / "In today's landscape"
- "It goes without saying"
- "The elephant in the room"
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's unpack this"
- "This is a game-changer"
- "Take it to the next level"
- "Move the needle"
- "Low-hanging fruit"
- "Circle back"
- "Double down"
- "The bottom line"
- "Food for thought"
- "A deep dive into"

## 5. Formatting Rules

- NO em-dashes (—). Use periods, commas, or colons instead
- NO perfectly numbered lists for everything
- NO headers for every 2-3 sentences
- NO bold on every other phrase
- Paragraphs should vary in length (2-6 sentences, not uniform)
